Top Egyptian Destinations

Cities, temples, and coasts — each card is a doorway into a different Egypt.

Capital

Cairo

The Egyptian Museum, Khan el-Khalili, and Coptic Cairo — a living city layered over pharaonic and Islamic history.

Highlight: Egyptian Museum & Citadel

Iconic

Giza

The Great Pyramid, Khafre, and Menkaure rise above the plateau, guarded by the Sphinx at the edge of the desert.

Highlight: Sound & Light show

Thebes

Luxor

Once the capital of the New Kingdom: Karnak, Luxor Temple, Hatshepsut, and royal tombs across the Nile.

Highlight: Valley of the Kings

Nubia

Aswan

Granite islands, colorful Nubian homes, Philae Temple, and the High Dam that reshaped the Nile.

Highlight: Abu Simbel day trip

Ramses II

Abu Simbel

Colossal rock-cut temples rescued from the rising waters of Lake Nasser — a UNESCO masterpiece in the deep south.

Highlight: Sunrise at the facade

Coast

Sharm El-Sheikh

World-class reefs, desert safaris, and Sinai sunsets — the Red Sea at its most photogenic.

Highlight: Ras Mohammed National Park

Mediterranean

Alexandria

Bibliotheca Alexandrina, Qaitbay Citadel, and sea-breeze corniche walks in Egypt’s historic port city.

Highlight: Catacombs of Kom El Shoqafa

Oasis

Siwa Oasis

Salt lakes, date palms, and the Oracle of Amun — a remote western oasis where time moves like desert wind.

Highlight: Cleopatra’s Spring
